### Added

- **`nest-rs-resilience`, behind the umbrella's `resilience` feature** (in
  `full`): a `Policy` named for the dependency it guards and shared by every
  caller of it — a per-attempt timeout cancelling the attempt's
  `CancellationToken`, an overall deadline, retries on the framework's jittered
  `Backoff` applied only to a call its caller declares idempotent
  (`run_idempotent`, `call().idempotent()`), a count-window circuit breaker with
  half-open probes, a bulkhead with a bounded queue, and a fallback
  (`run_or_else`). `failure_if` declares which errors are the dependency
  failing; the others end the call at once and count as successes.
- `ResilienceError<E>` implements `ToProblem` whenever `E` does: `TIMEOUT`
  (`504`), `CIRCUIT_OPEN` (`503` with `Retry-After`) and `BULKHEAD_FULL` (`503`)
  on every edge, the call's own error as it says.
- `PolicyConfig` reads a policy from configuration (milliseconds, unknown keys
  refused, every count and rate checked); `Policies` registers an app's policies
  by name and refuses a duplicate; `presets::{outbound_http, latency_critical,
  background}` start the common kinds of dependency.
- `Policy::stats()` counts calls, successes, failures, timeouts, retries,
  short-circuited calls, bulkhead refusals and breaker openings; a breaker
  transition is a `warn` on `nest_rs::resilience`.
- `nest_rs::reqwest::ResilienceMiddleware` (`nest-rs-reqwest`'s `resilience`
  feature, forwarded by the umbrella when both `reqwest` and `resilience` are
  on): an app's reqwest client sent through a policy, retried only for a method
  RFC 9110 names idempotent or a request carrying `Idempotency-Key`; a transport
  error, `408`, `429` and a `5xx` other than `501` count as failures.
- Repository hygiene: an `.editorconfig`, a `bacon.toml` the `.dockerignore`
  already named, and a `rustfmt.toml` pinning the edition the formatter reads.
