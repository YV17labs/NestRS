### Added

#### Resilience — a named policy around every call to a dependency

`cargo add nest-rs --features resilience` brings `nest_rs::resilience`: a
`ResiliencePolicy<I>` per dependency an app calls, named by an `#[instance]`
marker and built by `ResilienceModule::<I>`, which the module whose provider
injects it imports bare (`<PREFIX>_RESILIENCE__<INSTANCE>__*`, or
`ResilienceModule::for_root((Marker, ResilienceConfig { .. }))` to pin a base).
Every module importing one instance shares its one policy, so one breaker counts
the failures of every caller. ([Resilience](https://nestrs.dev/fundamentals/resilience/))

- **Every wait is bounded.** Each attempt is given up past `TIMEOUT_MS` and its
  `CancellationToken` cancelled; a retry waits `nest_rs_core::Backoff`'s jittered
  curve, from `BACKOFF_FIRST_MS` up to `BACKOFF_CEILING_MS`; a call waits
  `CONCURRENCY_WAIT_MS` at most for a slot of the bulkhead (`CONCURRENCY`).
- **A call is attempted again only where the caller declares it idempotent**:
  `run` sends it once, `run_idempotent` again after a failure or a timeout, up to
  `RETRIES`. Every `Err` counts as the dependency failing; an answer the
  dependency gave belongs in the call's `Ok`.
- **A circuit breaker** counts the latest `BREAKER_WINDOW` attempts, opens once
  `BREAKER_FAILURES` of them failed, refuses every call unsent for
  `BREAKER_OPEN_SECS`, then lets one probe through. Each transition is a `warn`
  on `nest_rs::resilience` and, beside the `opentelemetry` feature, one more on
  the counter `nestrs.resilience.breaker.transitions`, by
  `nestrs.resilience.instance` and `nestrs.resilience.breaker.state`.
- **A refusal is answered `503`, `UNAVAILABLE`, on every edge**: a timeout, an
  open breaker — with the `Retry-After` it knows — and a full bulkhead, as a
  `ResilienceError` implementing `ToProblem`, whose problem rides its chain, so
  a queue job failing with it waits at least that long.
- **`ResilienceMiddleware`, beside the `reqwest` feature,** runs the app's own
  reqwest client under a policy: it sends again what RFC 9110's method or an
  `Idempotency-Key` declares idempotent, on a transport failure, a timeout or a
  `408`, `429`, `500`, `502`, `503` or `504`, honouring a `Retry-After` up to the
  backoff's ceiling. Added before `ReqwestMiddleware`, each attempt is a client
  span of its own carrying `http.request.resend_count`.
- **A policy's state lives in its process**, each replica's breaker and bulkhead
  its own, and the page says so.

`reqwest-middleware` is now public in `nest-rs-resilience` too, at the `0.5`
`nest-rs-reqwest` already holds for 7.x.
