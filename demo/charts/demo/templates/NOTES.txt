{{- $worker := fromYaml (include "demo.appConfig" (dict "ctx" . "app" "worker")) }}
{{- if $worker.keda.enabled }}

The queue worker scales on each queue's stream length. Two facts to keep in view:

  - a scale-up moves no peer's jobs: a job changes hands only once the lease of
    the worker holding it lapsed — the queue is at-least-once, so keep handlers
    idempotent
  - at zero replicas nothing moves a retry or a delayed job onto the stream the
    trigger reads, nor recovers a dead pod's jobs, which is why
    minReplicaCount defaults to 1
{{- else if $worker.autoscaling.enabled }}

The queue worker scales on CPU. The demo's #[process] methods wait on I/O — four
transcodes and one notification write at a time per replica — so a backlog
leaves CPU flat and this HPA idle; queue depth, through KEDA, is the signal that
moves with the work (`apps.worker.keda`).
{{- end }}
