{{- if $worker.keda.enabled }}

The queue worker scales on Redis list length. Two facts to keep in view:

  - a worker start re-enqueues orphaned jobs across *all* registered consumers,
    so scaling up re-runs peers' in-flight jobs; `#[process]` must be idempotent
  - at zero replicas nothing promotes <queue>:scheduled or recovers
    <queue>:inflight, which is why minReplicaCount defaults to 1
{{- else if $worker.autoscaling.enabled }}

The queue worker scales on CPU. It runs one job at a time per #[process] method,
so a backlog of I/O-bound jobs leaves CPU flat and this HPA idle — queue depth,
through KEDA, is the signal that moves with the work (`apps.worker.keda`).
{{- end }}
